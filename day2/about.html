About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple and intuitive note-taking application designed to help you capture your thoughts and ideas quickly. Whether you're jotting down a quick reminder or brainstorming for a project, QuickNotes provides a seamless experience to keep your notes organized and accessible.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click the "Add Note" button to create a new note.
  2. Type your thoughts directly into the editor screen
  3. Click the "Save" button to store your note.

Features

  • Quick and easy note-taking
  • Organize your notes with tags
  • Search through your notes effortlessly

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
ctrl + N Create a new note
ctrl + S Save the current note
